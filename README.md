# POLYPHASE

**A familiar rhythm. A whole new dimension.**

An original falling-block arcade with five-square and six-square shapes. Built for that quiet moment when your hands find the rhythm and everything falls into place.

Double-click **PLAY.bat**, then choose your frequency. With the local server running, open [Polyphase](http://127.0.0.1:5173). Use headphones for the original adaptive soundtrack.

## Three frequencies

| Mode    | Shapes                       | Board   |
| ------- | ---------------------------- | ------- |
| Pentris | All 12 free pentominoes      | 12 × 22 |
| Sextris | All 35 free hexominoes       | 14 × 22 |
| Fusion  | A balanced mix of both sizes | 14 × 22 |

Each shape bag is shuffled without repetition. Fusion alternates shuffled pairs of piece sizes while keeping independent shape bags. Shapes can rotate; reflected variants count as the same free shape and are not separate bag entries.

Choose **Flow** for a gentle start or **Rush** for faster gravity. Every ten cleared lines increases the level. Chain line clears for combo bonuses. Fill your **Resonance** meter through placements and clears, then press Enter to dissolve the bottom four rows and give yourself breathing room.

## Feel every placement

- Luminous beveled blocks, landing guide, hold slot and four upcoming pieces.
- Shards, clearing beams, expanding shockwaves and impact shake.
- A GPU-rendered orbital halo, moving aurora, stars and drifting geometry.
- Three atmospheres: Eventide, Afterglow and Deep Blue, with matching original musical palettes.
- Procedural stereo music with pads, bass, arpeggios, drums and harmonized effects. Intensity responds to progress, combos and charge.
- Touch controls, fullscreen, local personal bests for each mode/pace, automatic pause when focus is lost, and reduced motion.
- Fonts, graphics and sound are local. No account, analytics, samples or runtime downloads.

## Controls

| Action                  | Keyboard              |
| ----------------------- | --------------------- |
| Move                    | Left / Right or A / D |
| Rotate clockwise        | Up, X or E            |
| Rotate counterclockwise | Z or Q                |
| Soft drop               | Down or S             |
| Hard drop               | Space                 |
| Hold / swap             | C or Shift            |
| Resonance               | Enter                 |
| Pause / resume          | P or Escape           |
| Mute / fullscreen       | M / F                 |

Mode and pace are locked during a run. Pause and choose **Back to frequencies** to start a different journey. Audio begins on the first play gesture. Controls and settings are also explained in the in-game help panel.
